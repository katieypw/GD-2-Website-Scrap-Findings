Scrap Findings
About

Scrap findings is a collective of found scraps that houses an archive of gestures and forms made through abandoned artwork. Connecting experience and materiality, this website is a reflection on finding imperfections and beauty in the making process. Scrap Findings is a love story told by the observer who connects experiences. All Scraps are scavenged by Katie Wu around OCAD University.
